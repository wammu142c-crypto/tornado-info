The Nature of Tornadoes

Atmospheric Dynamics, Formation, and the Enhanced Fujita Scale

How Tornadoes Form

Tornadoes are violently rotating columns of air extending from the base of a thunderstorm to the ground. Their formation relies on a precise sequence of atmospheric conditions:

  1. Moisture and Instability: Warm, humid air near the surface trapped beneath cold, dry air aloft creates unstable atmospheric conditions, forcing the warm air to rapidly rise.
  2. Wind Shear: Variations in wind speed and direction at different altitudes create an invisible, horizontal spinning effect in the lower atmosphere.
  3. Updraft Development: Rising air within a severe thunderstorm (supercell) tilts this spinning column of air from a horizontal position to a vertical position.
  4. The Mesocyclone: This rotating updraft, now miles wide, forms a stable, spinning core within the storm structure.
  5. Wall Cloud and Funnel: As the updraft intensifies, it draws in cool, moist air from the storm's downdraft, forming a low-hanging wall cloud. A condensed funnel cloud then descends toward the ground, officially becoming a tornado the moment it touches the surface or creates visible debris.

The Enhanced Fujita (EF) Scale

Tornadoes are classified using the Enhanced Fujita Scale, which estimates wind speeds based on the structural and environmental damage left behind.

Rating Estimated 3-Sec Gusts Typical Damage Description
EF-0 65–85 mph Light. Peels surface off some roofs; some damage to chimneys; branches broken off trees; shallow-rooted trees pushed over.
EF-1 86–110 mph Moderate. Roof surfaces stripped; mobile homes overturned or badly damaged; loss of exterior doors; windows broken.
EF-2 111–135 mph Considerable. Roofs torn off well-constructed houses; foundations of frame homes moved; mobile homes completely destroyed; large trees snapped.
EF-3 136–165 mph Severe. Severe damage to large buildings; roofs and some walls torn off sturdy houses; trains overturned; most trees in forested areas uprooted.
EF-4 166–200 mph Devastating. Whole frame houses completely leveled; cars thrown considerable distances; large missiles and debris generated.
EF-5 > 200 mph Incredible. Strong frame houses leveled off foundations and swept away; automobile-sized missiles fly through the air over 100 meters; high-rise buildings suffer severe structural deformation.
